Frequently asked questions

Before entering the arena.

Is it a beauty contest?+

No. AuraPvP focuses on presence, style, safety, performance and the ability to engage the crowd during a live match.

Can I vote from home?+

No. Only people who checked in, stayed in the valid area for at least 70% of the battle and are still present when voting opens can vote.

Can other people see my location?+

No. Location is used only to verify presence at the event. Everyone else sees aggregate counts only.

Does paying increase the Aura I earn?+

No. Plans add battle credits, statistics and sharing options, but never change vote weight or the competitive result.

What happens if the minimum crowd is not reached?+

The battle can finish and remain in history, but it is marked invalid and does not change Rating, Aura Farmed, league or ranking.
